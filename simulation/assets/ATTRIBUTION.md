# original print geometry

the five original STL files are **turret mechanism** by **PJohnso1**, published april 3, 2020.

- source: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:4259522
- license: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- original files are preserved in `original/`. their hashes, triangle counts and bounds are in `manifest.json`.
- `assembled/` contains split, translated copies of the bushings and gears. the triangle surfaces are retained; units are converted from mm to m by the simulator.
- the simulated camera, adapter, simplified motors, drone and bird geometry are additions to this project.

the STL files describe printable parts, not a dimensioned assembly. the pan axis uses the base center. the tilt axis is at the upper bearing opening, approximately z=130.2 mm. shaft placement follows its print orientation and the bearing span. pinion placement and the camera adapter are assembly estimates, and need checking against the actual build. the virtual camera sits on the tilt shaft's load mount between the uprights, replacing the previous payload position.
